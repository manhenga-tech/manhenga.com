Preparing your site for a Starlink installation

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Guide
Connectivity1 November 20263 min readBy Manhenga Tech

A practical checklist for discussing the mounting location, indoor coverage and installation scope.

Start with the view of the sky

A suitable dish position is an important part of planning satellite internet. Starlink’s installation guidance explains that objects blocking the dish’s view can interrupt its connection. Use the obstruction tool in the Starlink app to assess a proposed position before agreeing a permanent installation.

  • Discuss the proposed mounting location and nearby trees or buildings.
  • Use the guidance for the specific hardware you plan to install.
  • Ask the installer to review access, mounting and cable routing.

Think about where you will use the connection

The dish position and the places where people use Wi-Fi are different parts of the same project. Tell the installer which rooms, offices or outbuildings need connectivity. This helps define what should be included in your network setup.

Make your quotation specific

Share your location, whether you already own a kit, and the work you need help with. Ask for the kit version, mounting accessories, installation work and activation assistance to be set out clearly. Confirm subscription and account arrangements with the service provider.

Your preparation checklist

  • Describe the site and the rooms that need internet.
  • Share the kit version if you already own one.
  • Discuss safe access with the installer rather than attempting roof work yourself.
  • Agree what equipment and support are included before work begins.
