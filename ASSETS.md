# Manhenga Tech image assets

The four service photographs were created with the built-in image generation tool for this website, then resized and compressed as WebP assets. They are illustrative service imagery, not photographs of claimed Manhenga Tech clients, staff or completed installations. No reference images were used.

The final project paths are:

- assets/images/manhenga/connectivity.webp
- assets/images/manhenga/network.webp
- assets/images/manhenga/security.webp
- assets/images/manhenga/support.webp

The Manhenga Tech SVG wordmarks and favicon are code-native brand artwork in the green and lime reference palette. The original Tekprof abstract hero background is retained as an alpha mask and tinted lime in CSS; decorative CSS shapes and icon fonts are retained from the uploaded template. Service photographs keep their natural colours.

## Final generation prompts

### Connectivity

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: technology business website photo, square composition. Create a professional editorial photograph of an unbranded rectangular white satellite internet dish, like a current flat Starlink antenna, securely mounted on a simple low-rise roof of a neat Zimbabwean small business, with a small solar panel visible on an adjacent roof in the background, bright natural daylight, grounded realistic roof mounting, warm neutral tones, subtle coral accents in environment, no people, no text, no company branding, no watermark. Center the dish with enough breathing room for the template's organic rounded image mask. This is illustrative stock imagery, not a claimed actual company installation.

### Network

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: technology business website photo, landscape composition. A close-up editorial photograph of a neatly organised compact business network cabinet with switches, router, blue Ethernet patch cables, tidy labels with no legible text. Realistic RJ45 sockets and sensible cabling, clean professional installation, navy, warm white and blue palette, soft natural light, shallow depth of field. No people, logos, brand names, text, or watermarks. Stock-style illustrative service imagery.

### Security

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: technology business website photo, landscape composition. A realistic white CCTV bullet camera installed on the corner of a clean light plaster wall at a Zimbabwean shop exterior, camera pointing toward a doorway/courtyard, neat waterproof junction box and cable conduit, understated warm daylight, navy and warm white palette, natural shadows, clear useful product detail, no people, logos, text or watermarks. Stock-style illustrative service imagery.

### Support

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: technology business website photo, landscape composition. An editorial close-up of the hands of a Black IT technician carefully checking a desktop computer motherboard at a clean workbench, a laptop and screwdriver nearby, professional competent setting in a small Zimbabwean IT workshop, warm natural light, navy and neutral palette. Only hands visible, no identifiable person, no company uniforms, logos, legible text or watermarks. Realistic components and hands. Stock-style illustrative service imagery.
