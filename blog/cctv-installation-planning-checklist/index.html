What to discuss before installing CCTV

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Guide
Security1 November 20263 min readBy Manhenga Tech

Define the views, recording needs and access arrangements that matter for your property.

Begin with the purpose of each camera

List the areas you want to monitor and what you need to see there. An entrance, counter, yard and storeroom may each need a different view. Share these priorities before a camera quantity is chosen.

  • Which entrances or work areas need coverage?
  • What activity should be visible in each view?
  • Are there existing cameras or recording equipment to retain?

Discuss daylight and night-time conditions

A picture that looks useful in daylight may need different consideration at night. Axis’s image quality guidance recommends checking camera performance under the lighting and movement expected at the site. It also explains how strong differences in brightness and reflections can affect the image. Include these conditions in your installation discussion.

Plan recording and access

Explain your recording requirements and who should be able to view the system. Ask how footage is reviewed and exported, which devices will use remote viewing, and what equipment is included. Discuss the views with the people responsible for the property so cameras are aimed at the intended areas.

Agree the handover

A useful handover should cover the agreed camera views, recording checks and remote viewing setup. Keep the equipment details and support contact. If your needs change later, this record makes the next maintenance or expansion discussion easier.
