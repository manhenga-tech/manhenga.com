Planning Wi-Fi for a home, shop or school

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Guide
Networking1 November 20263 min readBy Manhenga Tech

Start with your rooms, users and daily work before choosing the equipment.

Map the places that need service

Begin with a simple layout of the property. Mark classrooms, offices, counters and other spaces where people need connectivity. Include outdoor areas and separate buildings if they are part of the requirement.

  • Where do people work, learn or serve customers?
  • Which applications and devices do they use?
  • Where does the existing connection stop being useful?

Check the actual environment

Wireless coverage depends on the environment, so equipment placement needs more than a guess. Cisco Meraki’s site survey guidance recommends assessing the site before and after deployment. Its guidance also highlights building materials and interference as factors to consider. A survey helps make equipment and placement decisions specific to the property.

Describe the busiest part of the day

A quiet room and a full classroom have different requirements. Explain the number of users, the devices they bring and the tasks that matter most. Tell the installer whether guest access, a hotspot or vouchers are part of the plan.

Agree how the setup will be checked

Ask for the agreed coverage areas and the installation scope to appear in the quotation. After installation, check the areas you identified using the devices and applications you actually use. Keep a record of the equipment and contact details for ongoing support.
